The History of Halloween

Halloween is a holiday celebrated every year on October 31st. Its history can be traced back to over 2,000 years ago as the ancient Celtic Festival of Samhain. People wore costumes and lit large bonfires to war off or hide from roaming spirits.

In the 8th century, Pope Gregory III designated November 1st as All Saint's Day. The night before became known as All Hallows' Eve, eventually known as Halloween. Medieval customs like "souling" involved the poor offering prayers for food treats, laying groundwork for modern practices.

Over time, Halloween developed into a holiday associated with costumes, decorations, pumpkins, and telling scary stories. Today, people celebrate Halloween by dressing up, decorating their homes, and participating in traditions such as trick-or-treating.
