Pumpkins

The pumpkin is one of the most recognizable symbols of Halloween. During Halloween, pumpkins are often carved to express faces or designs, turning them into Jack-O-Lanterns. They are then placed outside homes as decoration. They help create the spooky atmosphere associated with Halloween. People even place lights or candles inside so they glow.

Beware of the pumpkins after dark...
